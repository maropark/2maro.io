Vol. I · No. 01 — A Field Guide — Independently Composed

A design-practice piece: the front page of a newspaper, rebuilt for a market that never closes. Warm paper by day, a Bloomberg terminal after hours — one live accent travels with your attention the whole way down. Every component below is demonstrated while it is named.

01 — Ink & Paper

Two surfaces, three inks — everything else is one accent.

Warm newsprint paper, warm ink, hairline rules. That's the entire neutral palette. Colour is spent exactly once — on the figure and its kicker, where it counts. There are no boxes with shadows competing for attention.

The page never cuts to dark. It blooms between paper and a night canvas — that's the After Hours pole, chapter 08.

The neutral palette
The traveling accent — hover a desk to retune the page

There is exactly one live accent at any instant, held in two tokens — --bs-glow reads on paper, --bs-glow-soft blooms on night. It retunes instantly, no tween: the change is the event, not a lag behind your cursor.

02 — The Voices

Three typefaces, never blurred.

Newsreader carries the editorial voice — headlines, body, and figures. A number is set in the same serif as the sentence around it: the figure is editorial, not a UI stat. Archivo is the chrome — kickers, labels, nav, buttons. JetBrains Mono is telemetry only: the ticker, IDs, timestamps.

03 — The Grid

Rules over boxes, hairlines over shadows.

Newsprint geometry: clear measures, hairline dividers, generous gutters, corners barely rounded. Drop-shadowed cards are the SaaS trope this system rejects — a 1px hairline or a 2px strong rule does the same job of separation, honestly.

Each card carries its desk's hue as a stripe — a 2px top border on the front page, a 3px left border on the league table. That stripe is the only chrome a card gets.

04 — The Wire

The ticker never stops.

You've been reading it since scroll zero — the Edition Bar's live-wire status up top is real chrome, not a mockup. Below, the full Bloomberg-style telemetry marquee: domain dot, company, headline figure, desk. Pauses on hover so you can actually read one.

05 — The Dispatch

The page assembles itself.

On load and on filter change, the front page composes top-to-bottom — each card rises on a staggered delay so it "prints." Hover a card and the page retunes to its desk, not the card itself — no transform, no tilt, just the accent following your attention.

06 — The League Table

Same kit, airier composition.

The Dispatch edition — the airy alternative to the packed front page. A wire-service header carries the aggregate impact; below it, a ranked index sorted by projected impact, hue-spined on the left, figures flush right. Bloomberg terminal, calmed down.

07 — Humanizing the Number

The system never prints the enum.

A raw record arrives as structured data — an impact_type, maybe a value, maybe a dollar figure. The resolver below turns that into one of three display kinds, phrased the way an analyst would actually write it. Cycle the states — notice the last one never fakes a number.

08 — After Hours

The band blooms to night as it crosses center.

One value — --c, 0 to 1 — mixes paper→night and ink→bone as this band scrolls through the middle of your viewport. Two soft hue blobs drift behind it in screen blend for aurora. This is where the governance story lives: board and enterprise dispatches, in the domain's glow tone instead of ink.

After Hours §08 · the boardroom · the enterprise

09 — The Dossier

The full article, honestly sourced.

Open any card above, or the one below, and it turns to its dossier — a single reading column, the hero figure bracketed by rules, sources listed as first-class furniture. External sources get a link and an arrow; internal ones are labeled, never disguised. No sources cited means exactly that — the system doesn't fake proof.

10 — The System

The whole paper, one page.

A condensed reference — palette, desks, type, the six motions, four voice principles. Flip the switch below to preview the page with motion off: the Desk keeps naming every chapter, the wire goes static, everything else stays exactly as legible.
