01 · Philosophy 理念

More than a salon. A sanctuary.

MEGUMI is more than just a salon. It is a sanctuary where ancient wisdom meets modern science — where nature's grace and scientific precision unite to unlock your hair's true potential.

古 · Ancient wisdom

We believe true beauty is born from health, and healthy hair begins with a healthy scalp. Care, drawn from carefully selected botanical ingredients and the quiet disciplines of Japanese hair craft.

科 · Modern science

Every ritual begins with a careful scalp analysis, then evidence-based techniques tuned to you — a personalized design and a home-care routine built on what your scalp actually needs.

(01) “We don't just transform your hair — we restore balance to your mind and body.”

The head spa as a ritual of renewal.

A moment to pause, breathe deeply, and reconnect with yourself. Close your eyes — we'll read what your scalp has been trying to tell you.

    Scalp analysis

    診る
