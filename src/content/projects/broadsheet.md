BROADSHEET is a personal design-practice piece: what happens when a newspaper's
front page is rebuilt for a market that never closes. Warm newsprint paper,
warm ink, hairline rules — and exactly one live accent that travels instantly
to whatever has your attention, no tween. As you scroll, a bottom-fixed
instrument strip (The Desk) names the active chapter, its mechanical spec, and
live readouts — the wire ticker, the dispatch grid, the league table, the
impact humanizer, the After Hours polarity bloom, and the dossier detail view
are all demonstrated live, not just described.

Same discipline as KINESIS — self-demonstration over static documentation —
retuned to an entirely different register: an analyst's calm instead of a
dream maze. Every dispatch, company, and figure on the page is fictional demo
data, built to exercise every component honestly. Plain HTML, CSS &
JavaScript, one vendored dependency (Lenis), no build step, and a first-class
`prefers-reduced-motion` path with its own live in-page toggle.
